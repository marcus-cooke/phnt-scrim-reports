# Phantom Regiment brand

Reference: https://phantomwardogs.com/ — inspected 25 October 2026.

The emblem and PHANTOM wordmark are the original, unmodified SVG files from the official site's `/media/` directory. The favicon is the original site's SVG favicon. Fonts are the exact WOFF2 files served by the reference site; their OFL notices are included in `fonts/`.

- Headings: **Oxanium 800**, uppercase, 0.01em tracking.
- Navigation: **Oxanium 600**, uppercase, 0.08em tracking.
- Buttons: **Oxanium 700**, uppercase, 0.08em tracking; chamfered primary action.
- Body: **Instrument Sans 400**, with 500/600 for emphasis and controls.
- Signal red: **#D52B27**; phantom white: **#F1F1ED**; black: **#050505**.
- Graphite background: **#14171A**; surfaces: **#1A1E22** and **#22272C**.

The report keeps its data workspace and comparison structure. Red accent marks, the original logo lockup and the official typography provide the identity. Hover and focus changes remain restrained; reduced-motion preferences are respected.

Blue, Red and Green chart colours encode game factions and retain their original meaning. Branding never recolours source screenshots or changes any match data.
